# InStoreLook — VC Demo Video Voiceover

**Length:** approximately 32 seconds  
**Audience:** venture capital / early-stage investors  
**Tone:** confident, clear, practical

## 0:00–0:05 — The idea

“InStoreLook makes local fashion discoverable. It helps shoppers see what independent stores actually have nearby, before they leave home.”

## 0:05–0:10 — The problem

“Today, shoppers cannot reliably search local inventory. They lose time visiting stores one by one, while great independent retailers remain invisible online.”

## 0:10–0:15 — Shopper journey

“The experience starts with the shopper’s current location. InStoreLook surfaces nearby stores, map details, distance, contact information, and directions.”

## 0:15–0:20 — Product discovery

“A shopper can open a product, see the Indian rupee price, current stock, sizes, material, and the exact store carrying it.”

## 0:20–0:25 — Store visit

“With one tap, they can call the store, open directions, or continue browsing the store’s live catalog.”

## 0:25–0:30 — Retailer workflow

“For retailers, one photo upload becomes a structured listing. AI assists with titles, fabric, color, descriptions, and pricing, while the seller keeps final control.”

## 0:30–0:35 — Business opportunity

“We sit between maps and marketplaces: maps show where a store is; InStoreLook shows what is available now.”

## 0:35–0:40 — The ask

“The next step is to seed Hyderabad with local retail inventory, onboard the first sellers, and measure discovery-to-store visits. We are building the operating system for local fashion discovery.”

## Live-demo order for the presenter

1. Open the app and allow location permission.
2. Point out the detected Indian city and ₹ pricing.
3. Open **Nearby Stores**.
4. Show a nearby store’s address, landmark, phone, and directions.
5. Open a product and show price, stock, sizes, and store.
6. Switch to the seller flow.
7. Upload a product photo and show AI-assisted listing.
8. End with the business model and funding ask.
